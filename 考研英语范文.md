# 作文素材总结 — 英语二高分范文


## 1. 文章到底给了什么
- **小作文 15篇完整范文**（每篇约120词，三段式）：
  1.建议信 2.投诉信 3.道歉信 4.感谢信 5.邀请信 6.推荐信 7.祝贺信 8.求职信 9.辞职信 10.咨询信 11.寻找失物信 12.倡议信 13.致编辑信 14.告示 15.备忘录
  > 每篇结构固定：第一段=表面身份+表明主题；第二段=展开主题+两点论(First and foremost / What's more)；第三段=再扣主题+寒暄套话(Yours sincerely / Looking forward)

- **大作文 1套万能模板 + 4种图表描述**（建议150词，三段7-9句）：
  - 第一段：引出主题(What is illustrated...) + 描述数据(A/B/C/D四版本) + 过渡句(Simple as... epoch-thinking)
  - 第二段：重申原因句(There are many factors...) + 原因1(Initially... economic development...) + 原因2(At the same time... technology...)
  - 第三段：升华主题(Considering all these...) + 措施句(政府/媒体/个人三选一) + 展望(Only in this way / As far as I am concerned...)

- **万能理由库 5类10句**：经济/教育文化/环境保护/网络游戏/社会热点（养老），直接套进第二段原因句

- **2022真题示范**：快递业务量柱状图(2018-2020农村12→30亿，总体51→83亿)，用同一模板写出226词范文

- **备考建议**：12月开始、小作文日更1篇、大作文背理由不背整篇、默写3遍、字迹工整比连笔重要

## 2. 最关键的结论
- **不要15篇全背**：英语二小作文近10年只考了建议信、邀请信、道歉信、感谢信、祝贺信、告示。投诉/辞职/咨询几乎不考MBA。
- **大作文不要10篇**：图表就4种(柱/线/饼/表)，但描述句 A/B/C/D 选一套背熟即可，考场只填数字。
- **得分点在第二段**：阅卷看两点论 + 逻辑词，理由写得像“经济+科技”是万金油，比背偏门范文有效。
- **提分关键**：模板未固化+考场现编是丢分主因。把模板默到肌肉记忆，阅读不用加时。

## 3. 作者推荐背法
每天1篇小作文 + 每周2个万能理由，考前在答题卡纸上默写，查错词+练版式。作文课解决“怎么套”，高分范文解决“背什么”。
# 大作文 150词 挖空模板（1套通吃 柱/线/饼/表）

> 目标：背1套，不是背10篇。考场只改 XXX 和数字。原文226词偏长，考试压到170词左右最安全。

## 第一段 3句（图表描述）
1. `What is illustrated in the [column chart/line graph/pie chart/table] clearly shows that [主题词 e.g. the change in business volume of express delivery in China from 2018 to 2020].`
2. 选一句背熟就行（推荐A万能版）：
   - A版(通用)：`To be specific, [A] accounts for the highest proportion at [X%] of [主题词], followed by [B] at [Y%] and [C] at [Z%]. At the bottom, [D] is regarded as [W%].` 
   - C版(有变化)：`Meanwhile, there is a sharp increase from [5%] to [X%] between [2010] and [2020] in [主题词].`
   - D版(时间段)：`The past few years between [2018] and [2020] witnessed a sharp increase in the number of [museums/express delivery] from [4160/51 billion] to [4690/83 billion].`
3. `Simple as the [chart/table] looks, its implicit meaning should be taken seriously.` (原文 epoch-thinking 拼写怪，改成 taken seriously 更安全)

## 第二段 3句（万能原因，直接抄理由库）
1. `There are many factors contributing to this phenomenon, but overall, they may be put down to the following two aspects.`
2. `Initially, there is no doubt that with the rapid economic development in recent years, people's living standards have been improved remarkably, as a result of which, an increasing number of people are likely to [online shopping / pay more attention to XXX].`
3. `At the same time, along with the prosperity of technology and the advancement of the Internet, a growing quantity of [people/students]'s [reading/shipping] habits have been changed in that mobile phones would provide more convenience, which should be taken into account.`

> 替换库（第二段第2/3句可替换为万能理由）：
> - 教育类：`Education is becoming a lifetime practice... beneficial not merely to personal growth but to economic prosperity.`
> - 环境类：`The process of industrialization and urbanization has generated severe pollution that essentially destroys environments and ecosystems.`
> - 社会热点：`The government, society and children should care for the old together... reflects the degree of civilization.`

## 第三段 3句（升华+措施+展望）
1. 选立场：
   - 正面：`Considering all these contributors, we should bear in mind that great importance should be attached to [主题词].`
   - 负面：`Judging from the analysis above, we may safely come to the conclusion that the severe problem demands effective counter-measures.`
2. 措施三选一：
   - 个人：`Everyone should cultivate a better awareness of it and take actions.`
   - 政府：`Implementation of relevant measures and regulations concerning [主题词] should be underway.`
   - 媒体：`Newspaper and news media should launch propagandas of it.`
3. `Only in this way would our lives become more colorful and meaningful. / As far as I am concerned, it is a positive trend and should be stimulated, since it is conducive not merely to individuals but to the whole society.`

# 小作文 4封必背 + 11封替换（120词 三段式）

> 15篇全背=死记硬背，4封必背+1个万能替换=考场通吃。先把这4封默到0错别字。

## 通用信头信尾（所有类型共用，背1次）
```
Dear Sir or Madam, / Dear Professor, / Dear Mr. Manager, / To Whom It May Concern,
（正文每段前空2格=敲2个空格再写）

Yours sincerely,          ← 右对齐
Li Ming                   ← 右对齐
```

## 第1封：建议信（最高频，2011/2018考过，MBA最爱考）
**第一段**：`On behalf of the Students' Union, I am writing to extend the warmest welcome to overseas students. / I'm writing, after careful consideration, to present some suggestions about [XXX].`
**第二段**：`To help you fit into collegiate life, I'd like to offer some practical suggestions. First and foremost, it is highly proposed that you engage in activities organized by the Students' Union, so as to get acquainted with those from diverse backgrounds. What's more, you are advised to attend lectures delivered by professors, which will definitely broaden your horizons.`
**第三段**：`Thanks for your attention and it is hoped that the proposals above could be of help. / I hope you will find the proposals useful and wish you a fulfilling college life.`

## 第2封：邀请信 / 感谢信（二合一，改首尾即可）
**邀请版第一段**：`I am writing this letter in purpose of inviting you to [attend a meeting / be a judge].`
**感谢版第一段**：`I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for your timely help / generous hospitality.`
**第二段共用**：`If it had not been for your timely assistance in [giving me first aid / ...], I fear the consequences might have been serious. Everyone agrees that it is your quick-witted response that has led to this satisfactory outcome.`
**第三段**：`In the end, I would like to extend my sincere appreciation again. If there were more people like you, this world would be a nicer place. / I look forward to your coming.`

## 第3封：道歉信（考过多次，2023考过）
**第一段**：`I am writing to express my sincere apology for that I will not be able to make the interview at 10:00am on Monday, December 25.`
**第二段**：`The primary reason why I will be absent is that I will take a dental surgery, the dentist required me to take it as soon as possible to prevent further deterioration. If possible, I would like to postpone it to another day later in the week. I promise such a situation does not usually happen and I will attend punctually next time.`
**第三段**：`Once again, I feel really sorry for any inconvenience caused. Hope you can accept my apologies and understand my situation.`

## 第4封：投诉信 / 告示（投诉改告示只改第一句）
**投诉版第一段**：`As a regular customer, I am severely disappointed to find that the service you provided is far from satisfactory. Consequently, I am writing to complain and hope the situation can be improved.`
**告示版第一段**：`The Students' Union is going to hold [a low-carbon campaign / ...] and I am writing to call on all students to participate.`
**第二段投诉**：`First and foremost, punctuality is essential to railway service. However, the train has been delayed from time to time, which has brought me much inconvenience. What's more, the service of your staff has not benefited from "The customer is king."`
**第二段告示(倡议)**：`To help students develop a low-carbon lifestyle and advocate energy efficiency, we will hold lectures to introduce the concept. What's more, we will hand out leaflets to every dormitory, recommending students to switch off unused lights.`
**第三段投诉**：`I believe you will take this matter into serious consideration and give a satisfactory reply as soon as possible. Looking forward to your reply.`
**第三段告示**：`All members are required to take part. Other creative ideas will be encouraged.`
