Day4 投诉信（120词）- 必背原文

Dear Mr. Manager,

As a regular customer of your company, I am severely disappointed to find that the service you recently provided is far from satisfactory. Consequently, I am writing to complain about an unpleasant experience in the hope that the situation can be improved.

First and foremost, punctuality is essential to railway service. However, the train has been delayed from time to time, which has caused me considerable trouble and inconvenience. What is more, the service provided by your staff has not lived up to the saying, “The customer is king.”

I believe that you will take this matter into serious consideration and give me a satisfactory reply as soon as possible. I sincerely hope that you will review your management system.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

素材修正记忆点：service / disappointed to find / in the hope that / staff / live up to / considerable inconvenience。

格式：每段前空2格，手写时 Yours sincerely 和 Li Ming 靠右。

Day4 阅读精读（20分钟）- 2019年英语二 Text 2 真题

真题信息：2019年全国硕士研究生招生考试英语（二），阅读理解 Text 2，主题为“森林碳汇、气候变化与加州森林计划”。原文出处为 Bloomberg，2017年11月28日。

说明：公开解析页提供真实题目、选项、答案和解析，但未完整刊载文章正文；完整原文以纸质真题或正版真题资料为准。本页不使用自编文章替代原文。

原文主题：

文章讨论气候变化如何削弱森林吸收二氧化碳的能力，并介绍加州通过降低部分森林密度、清理灌木和控制燃烧来降低火灾与干旱风险，从而保护森林的长期碳汇功能。

5题：

1. “One of the harder challenges” implies that ______.

A. global climate change may be out of control B. forests may become a potential threat C. people may misunderstand global warming D. extreme weather conditions may appear

2. To maintain forests as valuable “carbon sinks”, we may need to ______.

A. preserve species diversity B. reduce their current carbon-absorbing capacity C. accelerate the growth of young trees D. strike a balance among different plants

3. California’s Forest Carbon Plan endeavors to ______.

A. restore forests quickly after wildfires B. grow more drought-resistant trees C. find more effective ways to control insects D. reduce the density of some forests

4. What is essential to California’s plan according to Paragraph 5?

A. Sufficient financial support B. Implementation before 2020 C. Priority treatment of the most risky areas D. Improvement of emission-permit auctions

5. The author’s attitude toward California’s plan can be best described as ______.

A. supportive B. ambiguous C. tolerant D. cautious

Day4 答案&解析（做完再点开）

答案： 1. B　2. B　3. D　4. C　5. A

1. B：气候变化可能使森林从碳汇变成碳排放源，森林因此可能成为潜在威胁。

2. B：为保持森林长期的碳汇功能，短期内可能需要通过疏伐降低当前碳吸收量，以降低火灾和干旱风险。

3. D：加州计划包括疏伐幼树、清除灌木和控制燃烧，核心是降低部分森林的植被密度。

4. C：计划应优先处理最容易遭受火灾或干旱的高风险区域。

5. A：作者认为该计划值得肯定，并可为其他地区提供参考，整体态度是支持的。

长难句： To maintain forests as valuable carbon sinks, we may need to reduce their current ability to absorb carbon. → 为了让森林继续成为有价值的碳汇，我们可能需要降低它们当前吸收碳的能力。To maintain... 作目的状语，as valuable carbon sinks 表示“作为有价值的碳汇”。

核心词： carbon sink 碳汇 / carbon-absorbing capacity 吸收碳的能力 / threat 威胁 / drought 干旱 / density 密度 / wildfire 野火 / priority 优先事项 / emission 排放
