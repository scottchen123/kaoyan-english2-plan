Day3 道歉信（120词）- 必背原文

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my sincere apology for my inability to attend the interview scheduled for 10:00 a.m. on Monday, December 25.

The primary reason for my absence is that I have to undergo dental surgery on that day, as my dentist has advised me to receive treatment as soon as possible to prevent further deterioration. If possible, I would like to postpone the interview to another day later in the week. I promise that such a situation will not happen again, and I will attend punctually next time.

Once again, I am truly sorry for any inconvenience caused. I hope you can accept my apology and understand my situation.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

素材修正记忆点：to express / inability to attend / undergo dental surgery / prevent further deterioration / attend punctually。

格式：每段前空2格，手写时 Yours sincerely 和 Li Ming 靠右。

Day3 四步清单（40分钟）

完成标准：核心句无语法错误，全文错词不超过2个，签名格式正确。

Day3 阅读精读（20分钟）- 2019年英语二 Text 1 真题

真题信息：2019年全国硕士研究生招生考试英语（二），阅读理解 Text 1，主题为“内疚感与儿童道德发展”。

说明：公开解析页提供真实题目、选项、答案和解析，但未完整刊载文章正文；完整原文以纸质真题或正版真题资料为准。本页不使用自编文章替代原文。

原文主题：

文章讨论内疚感对儿童成长的作用，指出内疚不只有负面作用，适度内疚能够帮助儿童理解社会规范，并通过同情和共情促进合作与分享。

5题：

1. Researchers believe that guilt can be a positive emotion because it may help children to ______.

A. regulate their basic emotions B. improve their intelligence C. enhance their positive feelings D. develop moral behavior

2. According to Paragraph 2, many people still regard guilt as ______.

A. deceptive B. addictive C. painful and burdensome D. unforgivable

3. Vaish believes that rethinking guilt comes from the realization that ______.

A. one emotion can produce opposite effects B. emotions can serve social purposes C. emotional stability is beneficial to health D. emotions are unrelated to circumstances

4. Malti and others' research suggests that cooperation and sharing may be ______.

A. ways to compensate for emotional deficiencies B. sources of emotional satisfaction C. prompted by sympathy or guilt respectively D. results of impulsive behavior

5. The word “transgressions” in the passage is closest in meaning to ______.

A. wrongdoings B. discussions C. restrictions D. instructions

Day3 答案&解析（做完再点开）

答案： 1. D　2. C　3. A　4. C　5. A

1. D：内疚能帮助儿童逐渐理解社会规范和道德规范，促进道德行为。

2. C：第二段把内疚描述为令人不舒服、沉重的情绪，故为 painful and burdensome。

3. A：Vaish 认为同一种情绪在不同情境下可能产生相反作用。

4. C：研究表明，同情和内疚可能分别促成合作与分享；respectively 表示“分别地”。

5. A：transgressions 指违反道德规范的行为，即过错、违规行为，最接近 wrongdoings。

长难句： One emotion can produce opposite effects in different circumstances. → 同一种情绪在不同情境下可能产生相反作用。

核心词： guilt 内疚 / moral 道德的 / emotion 情绪 / burdensome 沉重的 / sympathy 同情 / transgression 过错 / prompt 促使 / respectively 分别地
