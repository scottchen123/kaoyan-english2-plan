# 考研英语范文 - Day1 今日任务

> **今天1小时：建议信 + 改编阅读1篇。**

## Day1 建议信（120词）- 必背原文

Dear Sir or Madam,

  On behalf of the Students' Union, I am writing to extend the warmest welcome to overseas students.

  To help you fit into collegiate life, I'd like to offer some practical suggestions. First and foremost, it is highly proposed that you engage in activities organized by the Students' Union, so as to get acquainted with those from diverse backgrounds. What's more, you are advised to attend lectures delivered by professors, which will definitely broaden your horizons.

  Thanks for your attention and it is hoped that the proposals above could be of help.

<div style="text-align:right">Yours sincerely,<br>Li Ming</div>

## Day1 四步清单（40分钟）

- [ ] **1. 背 15分钟**：大声读3遍 → 盖住默写"欢迎—两条建议—盼采纳"
- [ ] **2. 默 15分钟**：白纸完整默写一遍，控制在15分钟内
- [ ] **3. 改 5分钟**：错词抄5遍 → `on behalf of / engage in / diverse / horizons / proposals`
- [ ] **4. 套 5分钟**：把 `overseas students` 换成 `new members`，把活动换成读书会

**完成标准**：代表身份、两条建议、盼采纳齐全，全文错词不超过2个，签名格式正确。

## Day1 阅读精读（20分钟）- 改编练习

> **说明**：本篇为改编练习（非真题），用于熟悉题型。从 Day3 起全部使用真题。

**原文：**

For many people, the idea of a "dream job" has changed. In the past, a secure job with good pay and benefits was ideal. Today, many young professionals prioritize work-life balance, meaningful work, and flexibility over salary. A survey of 2,000 graduates found that 68% would accept a lower salary for a job that offers more free time and personal fulfillment. Employers have noticed this shift and are now offering remote work and wellness programs to attract talent.

**4题：**

1. What did people value most in a dream job in the past?
    A. Flexibility  B. Security and pay  C. Meaningful work  D. Free time
2. According to the survey, what would 68% of graduates do?
    A. Ask for higher salary  B. Accept lower pay for more free time  C. Change their major  D. Start their own business
3. Why are employers offering remote work?
    A. To save money  B. To follow the law  C. To attract young talent  D. To reduce working hours
4. The passage is mainly about?
    A. How salaries have increased  B. The changing idea of a dream job  C. The benefits of remote work  D. How to find a job

- [ ] 18分钟做完，写下答案
- [ ] 对答案见折叠区

> [!NOTE]- Day1 答案&解析（做完再点开）
> **答案：** 1. B　2. B　3. C　4. B
>
> 1. **B**：首段 `a secure job with good pay and benefits was ideal`，过去看重稳定和高薪。
> 2. **B**：`68% would accept a lower salary for more free time`，接受降薪换时间。
> 3. **C**：`to attract talent`，吸引人才。
> 4. **B**：全文讲 dream job 观念变化。
>
> **长难句：** `many young professionals prioritize work-life balance ... over salary.` → `prioritize A over B` = 把A看得比B重要。
>
> **核心词：** `secure` 稳定的 / `prioritize` 优先考虑 / `flexibility` 灵活性 / `fulfillment` 成就感 / `shift` 转变

# 考研英语范文 - Day2 今日任务

> **今天1小时：感谢信/邀请信 + 改编阅读1篇。**

## Day2 感谢信/邀请信（120词，二合一）- 必背原文

Dear Professor,

  I am writing to express my heartfelt gratitude for your generous help / I am writing to invite you to attend our graduation ceremony next Friday.

  If it had not been for your timely assistance in giving me valuable guidance on my thesis, I fear that I could not have finished it on time. Everyone agrees that it is your profound knowledge and patient guidance that has led to this satisfactory outcome.

  In the end, I would like to extend my sincere appreciation again. If there were more people like you, this world would be a much nicer place. / I do hope you can accept my invitation and I am looking forward to your coming.

<div style="text-align:right">Yours sincerely,<br>Li Ming</div>

> **二合一记忆点**：第一段 `gratitude for...` ↔ `invite you to...` 二选一；第二段共用 `If it had not been for...` 句型；第三段 `appreciation again` ↔ `looking forward to your coming`。
> **格式**：每段前空2格，手写靠右。

## Day2 四步清单（40分钟）

- [ ] **1. 背 15分钟**：大声读3遍 → 盖住默 `I am writing to express... / invite you to... / If it had not been for...`
- [ ] **2. 默 15分钟**：白纸默一遍（邀请版和感谢版各默首段1遍）
- [ ] **3. 改 5分钟**：错词抄5遍 → `gratitude / generous / guidance / profound / appreciation`
- [ ] **4. 套 5分钟**：把 `thesis` 换成 `first aid / hospitality` 套一遍

**完成标准**：两版首段都会默，核心句 `If it had not been for...` 0错。

## Day2 阅读精读（20分钟）- 改编练习

> **说明**：本篇为改编练习（非真题），用于熟悉题型。从 Day3 起全部使用真题。

**原文：**

In recent years, many companies have begun to use AI to screen job applications. The software scans resumes for keywords and selects candidates for interviews. Supporters say it saves time and reduces bias. However, critics argue that it may overlook talented people who describe their experience differently. It may also learn biases from past hiring data. As a result, some firms now use AI only as a helper, with a human making the final decision.

**4题：**

1. What does AI do in hiring according to Paragraph 1?
    A. It interviews candidates  B. It scans resumes for keywords  C. It writes resumes  D. It trains employees
2. What do supporters think of AI screening?
    A. It is time-saving and less biased  B. It is too expensive  C. It is unreliable  D. It is illegal
3. What is critics' concern?
    A. AI is too slow  B. AI may miss talents and learn biases  C. AI needs more data  D. AI will replace humans completely
4. What is the current practice of some firms?
    A. They abandon AI  B. They let AI decide alone  C. They use AI as assistant, human decides  D. They hire more staff

- [ ] 18分钟做完，写下答案
- [ ] 对答案见折叠区

> [!NOTE]- Day2 答案&解析（做完再点开）
> **答案：** 1. B　2. A　3. B　4. C
>
> 1. **B**：`scans resumes for keywords`，扫描简历关键词。
> 2. **A**：`saves time and reduces bias`，省时减偏见。
> 3. **B**：`may overlook talented people / learn biases`，错过人才、学到偏见。
> 4. **C**：`AI only as a helper, with a human making the final decision`，AI辅助、人类决策。
>
> **长难句：** `It may also learn biases from past hiring data.` → `learn from` = 从……中学习。
>
> **核心词：** `screen` 筛选 / `resume` 简历 / `bias` 偏见 / `overlook` 忽视 / `talented` 有才华的
